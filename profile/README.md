<h2 align="center">Timbre</h2>

<p align="center"><b>Your voice. Nobody else's.</b></p>

Timbre is a private voice studio for creators and small teams. It clones your
voice on your own computer, reads your scripts in it, and signs every clip you
export, so a clip that claims to be you can be checked.

- **Cloning happens locally.** A 30-second sample, opened by a spoken consent
  line, goes to an engine on your machine. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Scripts stay yours.** Projects live on your device or in a private,
  end-to-end encrypted space shared only with people you invite.
- **Clips carry proof.** Each export is watermarked, signed with Ed25519 and
  written to an append-only clip log that anyone can verify against.
